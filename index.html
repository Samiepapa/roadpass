RoadPass Privacy Policy

App: RoadPass: DMV Test in Korean (Android, package com.roadpass.korean)
Effective date: October 5, 2026
Developer: [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY NAME] · Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]

RoadPass is an unofficial study app that helps Korean speakers practice for the California driver's license written test. RoadPass is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the California Department of Motor Vehicles or any government agency.

1. Summary

  • There is no account, sign-up, or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Your study progress, wrong-answer notes, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects certain device information to show and measure ads. Subscribing removes ads.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, RoadPass saves the following on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

  • Study progress, answered questions, streak and daily goal.
  • Questions you answered incorrectly (wrong-answer notes).
  • Preferences you choose, such as vehicle type, exam date, first-try or retake, and whether the daily study reminder is on.
  • Whether your subscription is active (a cached copy of the status reported by Google Play).

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

3. Information collected by third-party services

Google AdMob (ads, free version only). The free version of RoadPass displays banner and interstitial ads using Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver, personalize (where permitted), measure, and protect against fraud in advertising. We do not receive your advertising ID or the data Google collects. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing (subscription). If you subscribe to remove ads, the purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. RoadPass receives only the subscription status (for example, "active") so it can turn ads off. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

Google User Messaging Platform (consent). Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), RoadPass asks for your consent before ads that use your data are shown, using Google's consent tool. Your choice is stored on your device.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: practice questions, mock tests, progress tracking, and reminders.
  • To show ads in the free version and to remove them for subscribers.
  • To send a local daily reminder notification if you turn it on. The reminder is scheduled on your device; it does not use a server.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to the above.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Ad privacy settings: where available, open Subscription tab → Ad privacy settings in the app to review or change your ad consent choice.
  • Advertising ID: on your Android device, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Google ad settings: manage personalized ads at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Remove ads: subscribe in the app to turn off ads.
  • Notifications: turn the reminder off in the app or in Android settings.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob, Google Play, consent tool), which process data as described above. We may disclose information if required by law, although as described, we do not hold personal data about you on our servers.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the data described in Section 2 lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling RoadPass or clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → RoadPass → Storage → Clear storage). We hold no copy. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies; see Section 3.

8. Children's privacy

RoadPass is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. People who are 15½ or older can take the California permit test, so some users may be teenagers. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us at the address below.

9. Security

Data stored in the app is kept in your device's private app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but because we do not transmit your study data to our servers, the risk of our systems exposing it is minimal.

10. Your rights

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising through Google AdMob can count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can opt out using the controls in Section 5.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland. You may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent for ads that use personal data. You can withdraw it through Ad privacy settings in the app.

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, most requests can be completed by the controls above. To make a request or ask a question, contact us at the address below.

11. "Do Not Track"

RoadPass is a mobile app and does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals. Use the Android advertising controls described in Section 5.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app or on this page.
